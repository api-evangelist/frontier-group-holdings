DENVER - Sept. 16, 2026 - Frontier Airlines (Nasdaq: ULCC) today announced the launch of Cancel for Any Reason, powered by HTS (Hopper Technology Solutions), giving customers even greater flexibility when making travel plans. Available when booking at FlyFrontier.com or through the Frontier mobile app, the new option allows customers to cancel up to 24 hours before departure and receive up to a full refund, depending on the refund option selected.
