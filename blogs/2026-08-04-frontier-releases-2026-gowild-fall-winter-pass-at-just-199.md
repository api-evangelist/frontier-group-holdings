DENVER – August 4, 2026 – Frontier Airlines ( Nasdaq: ULCC ) today launched its 2026 GoWild Fall & Winter Pass with immediate access, offering consumers the chance to purchase the pass now for just $199 and begin enjoying unlimited flights right away, rather than waiting for the typical pass period to begin. With the pass valid through Feb. 28, 2027, this means consumers who purchase now will enjoy nearly seven months of limitless travel with Frontier.
