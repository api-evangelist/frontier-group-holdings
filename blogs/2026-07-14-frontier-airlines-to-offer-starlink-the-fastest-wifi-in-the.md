DENVER – July 14, 2026 – Frontier Airlines ( Nasdaq: ULCC ) today announced it will transform the onboard experience by introducing Starlink, the fastest WiFi in the sky. Engineered by SpaceX, Starlink delivers an unparalleled broadband experience in flight, with high-speed, low-latency WiFi capable of HD streaming, online gaming, productivity and more. Frontier plans to launch its first Starlink-equipped aircraft in early 2027 and will be the first US airline to offer passengers access to Starlink’s high-speed internet through a new system managed directly by Starlink.
